## SLIDE 2: PROBLEM STATEMENT & POLAR VULNERABILITIES
* **Challenging Reality in Antarctica:**

  2. **Intermodal Cold-Chain Shock:** Irreplaceable scientific ice cores and pharmaceutical sera spoil if temperature fluctuates during transit.
  3. **Whiteout Crevasse Risks:** Field science convoys face life-threatening crevasse fall hazards without automated deadman timers.
  4. **Strict Treaty Mandate:** The Indian Antarctic Act, 2022 mandates statutory CAG-EP permits; Madrid Protocol Annex III mandates 100% waste return.
